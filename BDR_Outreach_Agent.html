Executive Summary

Cold email fails in predictable ways: too long, no clear ask, a made-up stat about the prospect, an opener that reads like a template. Each of those can be checked in code. This tool has a CrewAI researcher pick one angle from the prospect's notes, a writer draft the email, and plain Python checks run on every draft before it's saved. A failing draft goes back with the exact problems; after two retries it's saved marked FLAGGED for a rep to fix.

On a real run with qwen2.5 (7B), both drafts passed every check on the first attempt in 2 minutes 41 seconds total, at no model cost. Reading them as a rep would, each still needed one fix the checks can't see, which is why the output is a review file, not a send.

Real Run

What the drafts looked like

01Dana Whitfield, Controller at Northbeam Outdoor (fictional)

Notes given to the model: posted on LinkedIn that month-end close takes 9 days; hiring an AP specialist.

Subject: Streamline Month-End Closings with Ledgerline

Hello Dana,

I noticed you posted that month-end close at Northbeam Outdoor takes 9 days. Our Ledgerline solution automates accounts payable, which can help reduce this process time. Could I share more details on how we’ve helped other companies streamline their month-end close?

Best,  
Alex

What the checks confirmed

Subject under 7 words, body under 120, the company named, the "9 days" figure taken from her notes, exactly one question, no template opener, no banned words.

What a rep would still fix

"How we've helped other companies" hints at customer stories the product sheet doesn't contain. The prompt says never to invent customers; the model implied them instead, and a text check can't catch an implication.

02Marcus Oyelaran, VP Finance at Tidewater Clinics (fictional)

Notes: opened 3 new clinic locations this year; a job post mentions consolidating vendor payments. The draft passed, but it said the company is "focused on streamlining operations across multiple locations", which is vaguer than the fact it was given, and closed with a weak ask ("Have you considered this solution?").

03A fix carried over from the resume tailor

A real run of my AI Resume Tailor showed that "leveraging" could slip past a banned list containing "leverage", and that "[Company Name]" placeholders weren't checked. Both projects share the banned list, so the same fix and a test were added here.

Design

How it works

Account Researcher ──> Outbound Email Writer ──> guards ──┬─ pass ──> output/drafts.md + drafts.csv
  (one angle from the notes)   (subject + body)             └─ fail ──> retry with feedback (max 2), then FLAGGED
CheckWhat it catches
Invented numbersAny figure not in the prospect notes or product sheet ("15-minute call" is allowed)
PersonalizationThe body never names the prospect's company
One askExactly one question
Template opener"My name is...", "I hope this email finds you well" and similar
LengthSubject over 7 words, body over 120 words
StyleEm dashes, exclamation points, banned words and their variants, placeholders
